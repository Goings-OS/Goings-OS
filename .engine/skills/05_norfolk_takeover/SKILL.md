## 🎯 Section I: Executive Summary & Event Positioning

The Norfolk Takeover is the premier group cruise experience sailing out of Norfolk and East Coast ports. It unites hundreds of travelers from Hampton Roads and beyond for an all-inclusive voyage featuring exclusive private deck parties, themed banquets, VIP excursions, and curated onboard entertainment.

### Core Campaign Objectives
* **Stateroom Cabin Conversions:** Driving direct cabin bookings across Balcony, Oceanview, and Interior stateroom tiers.
* **Flexible Deposit Payment Schedules:** Structuring low initial deposit triggers paired with automated monthly installment reminders.
* **VIP Experience Upgrades:** Selling private cocktail receptions, exclusive shore excursions, and limited-edition merchandise packages.
