## 📋 Section II: Financial Terminology & Compliance Governance

* **Owner's Draw Mandate:** All shareholder distributions outside of W-2 payroll must be strictly designated as an "owner's draw".
* **Entity Segregation:** Maintain strict separation between operating entities (Keep It Goings LLC, TBE Shield, Luxury Affairs, Choice Inc).
* **Statutory Rigor:** Reference applicable Internal Revenue Code (IRC) sections accurately without offering formal individualized CPA/attorney guarantees.
* **Typographical Standard:** Zero em-dashes permitted across all generated scripts and templates.
