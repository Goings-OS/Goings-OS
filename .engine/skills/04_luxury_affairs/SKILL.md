## 🎯 Section I: Executive Summary & Venue Positioning

Luxury Affairs Event Center is the premier luxury event and wedding destination in Hampton Roads. Located conveniently on Victory Boulevard in Portsmouth, the venue offers an upscale, customizable ballroom aesthetic paired with full-service staging, custom draping, VIP bridal suites, and premium lighting design.

* **Weddings & Receptions:** Elegant bridal staging, ceremony and reception combinations, chiavari seating, and ambient uplighting.
* **Corporate Galas & Networking:** Executive dinners, corporate award galas, holiday parties, and boardroom retreats.
* **Milestone Celebrations:** Milestone birthday galas, retirement banquets, baby showers, and anniversary galas.
* **Tiered Package Architecture:** Streamlined pricing structures ranging from venue-only rentals to all-inclusive luxury decor packages ($1,500 to $10,000 deposits).
