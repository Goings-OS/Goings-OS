## 🔒 Section II: Virginia State Bar (VSB) Compliance Guardrails

All generated video scripts, captions, and distribution payloads must strictly comply with the **Virginia State Bar Rules of Professional Conduct** (specifically Rules 7.1, 7.2, and 7.3 regarding legal advertising):

### 1. Mandatory Disclaimer Invariant
Every piece of published content featuring case discussions, legal commentary, or settlement numbers must include the official VSB disclaimer:
> *"Case results depend upon a variety of factors unique to each case. Case results do not guarantee or predict a similar result in any future case."*

### 2. Strict Prohibition on Outcome Guarantees
* Never state or imply that a specific legal outcome is guaranteed.
* Avoid superlative claims such as "the best lawyer", "guaranteed victory", or "we never lose".
* Frame all insights as legal education, procedural rules, and strategic perspectives.

### 3. Factual Accuracy & Jurisdiction Locking
* All legal procedures, statutory references, and code sections must cite the **Code of Virginia** accurately (e.g., Va. Code § 18.2-266 for DUI, Va. Code § 8.01-243 for Personal Injury statute of limitations).
* Clearly state principal office location: **Norfolk, Virginia**.
* Clearly identify the attorney responsible: **Eric Korslund, Esq.**
