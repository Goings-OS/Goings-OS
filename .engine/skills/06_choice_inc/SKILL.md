## 🎯 Section I: Executive Summary & Community Mission

CHOICE Inc. is a dedicated 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization committed to uplifting youth, supporting underserved families, and providing vocational pathways across Hampton Roads.

### Core Strategic Pillars
* **Youth Empowerment & Mentorship:** Providing at-risk youth with life skills, financial literacy, technology training, and positive leadership mentorship.
* **Workforce Development & Career Pathways:** Equipping young adults with practical job readiness, trade certifications, and entrepreneurship fundamentals.
* **Family Support & Community Advocacy:** Offering emergency family assistance, back-to-school drives, holiday food security, and community health resources.
* **Grant & Donor Stewardship:** Delivering transparent impact reporting to secure philanthropic foundation grants and individual corporate sponsorships.
