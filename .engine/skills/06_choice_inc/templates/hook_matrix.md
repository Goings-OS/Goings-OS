### Blueprint Y-01: Financial Literacy for Teens
* **Hook (0 to 3s):** "What they do not teach our kids in school about money can keep them trapped in debt."
* **Community Tension (4 to 25s):** "Young people graduate high school knowing geometry, but have never been taught how credit scores work, how to invest in index funds, or how to avoid high-interest predatory loans."
* **CHOICE Inc Solution (26 to 45s):** "At CHOICE Inc, our youth financial literacy academy teaches teenagers real-world budgeting, smart banking, and credit building so they enter adulthood with financial power."
* **Call to Action (46 to 60s):** "Empower the next generation. Enroll your teenager or sponsor a student at CHOICE Inc today."
