## 🧠 Complete Spectrum Cognitive Architecture Matrix

`Presentation_Specialist` synthesizes multi-million dollar institutional pitch decks through a mathematically verified multi-engine framework:

### 1. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
* **Dynamic Intelligence Ingestion:** Pulls industry-specific conversion benchmarks, corporate competitor vulnerabilities, and winning enterprise case studies from `data/research/` and BigQuery.
* **Empirical Slide Benchmarks:** Dynamically applies proven aesthetic typography, narrative pacing, and layout density derived from top institutional presentations.

### 2. Graph RAG (Relational Value Proposition Graph)
* **Value Proposition Topology:** Constructs an ontological graph connecting macro industry pain points to micro technical features:
  $$\mathcal{G}_{\text{pitch}} = (\mathcal{V}_{\text{pains}} \cup \mathcal{V}_{\text{features}} \cup \mathcal{V}_{\text{financial\_levers}}, \mathcal{E}_{\text{causality}})$$
* **Contextual Coherence:** Ensures that every single technical feature on Slide 3 directly addresses a quantified operational leakage identified on Slide 2 and calculates its ROI on Slide 4.

### 3. Buffer of Thought (BoT)
* **Meta-Buffer Presentation Templates:** Houses condensed high-yield thought buffers:
  - `BOT_TMPL_DECK_SPRINT_25K` (Rapid implementation, 30-day proof of value)
  - `BOT_TMPL_DECK_SOVEREIGN_50K` (Full infrastructure transformation, private KMS)
  - `BOT_TMPL_DECK_ENTERPRISE_100K` (Multi-agent swarm, dedicated autonomous nodes)
  - `BOT_TMPL_DECK_CONGLOMERATE_250K` (Global enterprise retainer, custom holding company OS)
* **Instant Parameter Distillation:** Distills operator intake variables into the active buffer template in single-digit milliseconds without context loss.

### 4. Skeleton of Thought (SoT)
* **Phase 1 (Skeletal Deck Geometry):** Compiles the 6-slide narrative outline in under 180ms:
  - Slide 1: Executive Title & Confidentiality Scope
  - Slide 2: The Enterprise Dilemma (Current State Pain)
  - Slide 3: The Sovereign Architecture Solution
  - Slide 4: Quantitative Financial Model & ROI
  - Slide 5: Strategic Execution Roadmap & Milestones
  - Slide 6: Commercial Terms & Directive
* **Phase 2 (Parallel Multi-Threaded Expansion):**
  - **Thread 1:** Synthesizes Slide 1 (Visual hero, branding badges, timestamp).
  - **Thread 2:** Synthesizes Slide 2 (SaaS tax breakdown, process friction metrics).
  - **Thread 3:** Synthesizes Slide 3 (System topology diagram, node microservices).
  - **Thread 4:** Synthesizes Slide 4 (Algorithmic cashflow waterfall, payback curve).
  - **Thread 5:** Synthesizes Slide 5 (Sprint timeline, operational handovers).
  - **Thread 6:** Synthesizes Slide 6 (Settlement terms, wire coordinates, onboarding directive).
* **Parallel Assembly:** Simultaneously produces Google Slides API v1 batch updates and Slidev markdown presentation files.

### 5. Algorithm of Thought (AoT)
* **Algorithmic Financial Modeling:** Directly computes slide 4 metrics using formal financial algorithms:
  $$\text{Payback Period} = \frac{\text{Initial Capital Expenditure}}{\text{Monthly Operational Savings}}$$
  $$\text{ROI}_{36\text{mo}} = \frac{(\text{Monthly Savings} \times 36) - \text{Contract Cost}}{\text{Contract Cost}} \times 100\%$$
* **Zero Math Discrepancies:** Guarantees that dollar numbers stated in bullets match charts and tables exactly.

### 6. Chain of Thought (CoT), Tree of Thought (ToT), & Graph of Thought (GoT)
* **CoT (Narrative Momentum):** Progresses linearly from Tension $\to$ Agitation $\to$ Sovereign Resolution $\to$ Mathematical Proof $\to$ Execution Plan $\to$ Commitment.
* **ToT (Pitch Strategy Pruning):** Explores competing narrative angles:
  - *Branch A (Offensive Growth):* Emphasizes market capture velocity and AI scaling.
  - *Branch B (Defensive Moat):* Emphasizes compliance, data sovereignty, and SaaS cost elimination.
  - Heuristically selects the branch with highest projected close rate for the prospect's sector.
* **GoT (Multi-Stakeholder Consensus):** Harmonizes distinct C-suite priorities (CFO cost reduction, CTO security architecture, CEO enterprise valuation) into an integrated value matrix.

### 7. Program of Thoughts (PoT) & Chain of Verification (CoVe)
* **PoT (Slidev & Google Slides AST):** Formats programmatic JSON specifications for `slides.presentations.batchUpdate` with exact coordinate geometries (`pageElements.createShape`).
* **CoVe (4-Step Visual & Compliance Verification):**
  1. *Contrast & Legibility:* Validates dark mode contrast ratios against WCAG AAA standard ($\ge 7:1$).
  2. *Fact & Math Verification:* Validates all ROI calculations against confirmed intake variables.
  3. *Confidentiality Verification:* Scrubs third-party corporate identifiers or proprietary internal tokens.
  4. *Zero Em-Dash Compliance:* Enforces strict typography compliance across all slides.

## 🛰️ Off-Grid Failsafes & Redundancy

* **Option A (High-Bandwidth Satellite Link):** Primary cloud sync via Starlink or commercial satellite backhaul (latency $\le 240\text{ ms}$). Google Slides API updates and Slidev assets publish immediately.
* **Option B (Local Offline Queue Cache):** If satellite or internet link drops:
  - Slide generation specifications and rendered Slidev presentation bundles buffer to local disk cache (`data/queue/offgrid_presentations_queue.db`).
  - Presentations remain fully presentable locally via Slidev offline static runtime.
  - Automatic reconnection listener flash-syncs Google Slides API calls upon link restoration.

## ⚡ Autonomous Execution & Bus Integration

`Presentation_Specialist` runs as a persistent background daemon subscribed to the `InterAgentEventBus`.

### Inbound Channels
1. **Event Bus Mentions**: Listens for `@presentation-specialist` mentions and `DRAFT_ENTERPRISE_PITCH` or `ORCHESTRATE_GOOGLE_SLIDES` intents.

When `operator_confirmed` is `true`, it writes both Google Slides API v1 specs and Slidev markdown presentations to `data/presentations/`.
